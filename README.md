# EdgeStudio.ai Feedback Hub 🚀

Welcome to the **EdgeStudio.ai** community! We are thrilled to have you as part of our beta program. Your feedback is the most important tool we have to build a platform that truly works for you.

---

### How can you help?

We’ve split our feedback into two simple paths so we can stay organized and fix things faster:

#### 1. Found a Bug? 🐛
If something is broken, crashing, or just acting weird, please submit a formal report so we can track it.
*   [**Click here to report a bug**](https://github.com/ieonai/edgestudio-feedbacks/issues/new/choose)

#### 2. Have an Idea or Question? 💡
This is the place for feature requests, general questions, or to discuss how you're using EdgeStudio.ai. We love hearing your ideas!
*   [**Go to the Discussion Section**](https://github.com/ieonai/edgestudio-feedbacks/discussions)

---

### Need help?
If you're not sure where your feedback fits, just head over to the [Discussions](https://github.com/ieonai/edgestudio-feedbacks/discussions) tab and start a new post. We monitor it daily!

*Thank you for helping us shape the future of EdgeStudio.ai!*
